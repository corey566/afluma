# Afluma Content Studio — Editor Guide

## Daily editing

Open `/admin` and use the left navigation. Most changes happen in **Pages**, **Media**, **Header**, **Footer**, **Products**, **People**, **Jobs**, and **Posts**.

## Page editor

A page uses approved components instead of an unrestricted page builder. Add, remove or reorder blocks such as Cinematic Hero, Bento Grid, Capability Theatre, Process, Media Feature, FAQ and CTA. This keeps all 1,669 pages consistent and responsive.

## Live preview

Use **Live Preview** in the editor to inspect desktop, tablet and mobile layouts. Draft changes remain private until published.

## Media

Upload media once, then reuse it. Complete alt text, source, creator, licence and production status. A video cannot be treated as production-approved until its rights and reduced-motion fallback are recorded.

## SEO

SEO indexing is intentionally guarded. A page stays noindex unless it is published, workflow-approved, and its evidence status is allowed. Insight articles also require at least one source, fact-check completion and originality review.

## Case studies

Client names, screenshots, quotations and results require permission. The CMS prevents indexing until permission and evidence are verified.

## Careers

Only publish a job when it is genuinely open. Closing or pausing a job removes it from public lists without deleting the record.

## Roles

- **Administrator:** users, integrations and all content.
- **Editor:** pages, structured content and publishing.
- **Author:** drafting and content updates.
- **Reviewer:** review, versions and approval support.
- **Media manager:** asset library and licences.
- **Recruiter:** jobs and careers content.

The roles are intentionally simple; normal editors do not need to manage technical settings.
